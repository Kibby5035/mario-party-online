# Mario Party Online

Mario Party 1, 2 and 3 (N64, USA) as native PC games with online play built in: everyone runs the game on their own
PC and only the controllers go over the internet, so it plays like one console. Up to 4 players.

## Download

Get **Mario Party Online.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest), extract it (right-click, Extract All),
and open `Mario Party Online.exe`. The launcher tells you when there's an update and installs it with one click.

You need your own ROM of each game you play (the N64 cartridge versions, USA): none are included. `HOW TO PLAY.txt` in
the zip explains hosting and joining.

## Built on

The PC versions are the community's static recompilations, with online play added:
[Mario Party: Recompiled](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp),
[Mario Party 2: Recompiled](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-2-recomp),
[Mario Party 3: Recompiled](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp), on N64Recomp, N64ModernRuntime,
RecompFrontend and RT64. GPL-3.0, as they are.
